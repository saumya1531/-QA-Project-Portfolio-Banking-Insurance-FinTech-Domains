# Customer 360 – Defect Log

## Purpose

This document demonstrates defect logging and defect management for a fictional Customer 360 application in a Banking/Financial Services environment.

## Sample Defects

| Defect ID | Defect Summary | Severity | Priority | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DEF-001 | Customer search returns incorrect customer record | High | High | Open |
| DEF-002 | Customer phone number is not displayed correctly | Medium | Medium | Open |
| DEF-003 | Account status is not updated after customer profile change | Medium | High | Retest |

## Defect Lifecycle

1. Defect identified during test execution
2. Defect logged with evidence and reproduction steps
3. Severity and priority assigned
4. Defect discussed during triage
5. Fix provided by development team
6. Defect retested by QA
7. Defect closed after successful verification

## Defect Fields

Typical defect information includes:

- Defect ID
- Summary
- Description
- Steps to reproduce
- Expected result
- Actual result
- Severity
- Priority
- Environment
- Status
- Evidence
- Retest result

## Note

These are fictional sample defects created for portfolio demonstration purposes. No production or confidential customer information is used.
